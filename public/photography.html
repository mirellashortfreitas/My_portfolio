Photography

A selection of my photography work, created as part of my GCSE coursework at Swindon College and exploring the theme of decay. Through still life, nature, architecture and everyday scenes, the series looks at how time, weather and neglect transform the things around us. Shot in both colour and black and white, the images draw on a wide range of forms and textures: brittle petals and spiky seed heads, rusted metal, lichen-covered stone, weathered wood, crumpled fabric and shriveled fruit. Contrasts of light and shadow, sharp detail and soft blur, and organic shapes against geometric structures highlight the quiet beauty that can be found in fading, weathered and forgotten subjects.
